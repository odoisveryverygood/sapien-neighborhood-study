# Project status

The current deliverable is a ten-second illustrative character performance test in `character-study/`. The earlier neighborhood prototype remains in the repository as a separate version.

## Implemented

- A continuous three-shot moment: compare a $540 offer and a written $450 limit, move the offer aside, release it and look up across the counter.
- Revised gaze and eyelid behavior, brow/lash detail, collar continuity, baked cloth movement, nail surfaces and relaxed hand motion.
- A runnable local browser player with playback, seeking, restart, fullscreen and source disclosure.
- A 1920 × 1080, 24 fps master; an actual browser recording; source code; rendering references; and credits.
- A full browser pass that decoded 240 source frames with zero dropped video frames and one startup waiting event. These are video-playback measurements.

## Not implemented or not established

- A live simulation, backend connection, explorable 3D world or interactive scenario execution.
- A complete character history or a connected journey through changing circumstances and settings.
- A definite chosen alternative or consequence. The paper gesture alone does not establish rejection or another final outcome.
- Population-level patterns, comparable scenario results or a research conclusion.
- Actual approved study records in the current character scene. The person, values and response are illustrative.
- Photographic close-up quality, broad emotional performance, dialogue, walking or vehicle interaction.
- A standalone reconstruction of the rendered scene from this checkout. The packed scene, raw assets, motion inputs and some prepared scenes are not included; see [rendering prerequisites](character-study/RENDERING.md).
- A hosted deployment or an official production release.

## Remaining work

1. Ground the experience in one clear research question and appropriate source records.
2. Establish the person's relevant history, needs and changing circumstances visually.
3. Show the alternatives encountered, an observable choice and what follows it.
4. Connect individual journeys to population patterns or a sourced finding, and support any scenario comparison with records from both conditions.
5. Resolve the close-up asset/performance limitations before expanding; validate each additional action and maintain identity, lighting and scale across settings.
6. Implement the agreed interaction model beyond replaying a movie, then audit the complete experience for clarity and source accuracy.

## Current review scope

The current test supports feedback on the character, staging and one limited gesture. It does not demonstrate the full World Models process. A working video player and passing playback checks do not establish cinematic realism or a completed research explanation.
