Character performance study · v02

One person compares a $540 monthly offer with a written $450 limit, moves the
offer aside, releases it and looks up across the counter. Three connected shots
show the same ten-second performance.

This is a focused visual study for review before expanding the sequence.
The person, amounts, circumstances and response are illustrative. No actual
Sapien participant record, motive, prediction or population result is claimed.

Compared with v01, the revision adds a readable view of the two amounts, a more
complete look-up/release/rest gesture, gaze limits with greater head involvement,
original brow and eyelash fibers that follow the face, and a baked cloth solve.
Eye materials, the collar seam and nail surfaces have been repaired. The hand
release now includes relaxed finger curvature and a small wrist turn.

The face and hair remain visibly computer generated. This is a restrained,
non-speaking performance; it does not validate dialogue, walking or vehicle
interaction. Paper remains a rigid prop moving on the counter. For a photographic
hero close-up, the recommended next production change is a filmed performer
under matching light; this character has not met that photographic standard.

Run node character-study/server.mjs from the repository root, or play
character-study/public/media/character-performance.mp4 directly.
The browser player replays pre-rendered 3D footage; it is not a real-time world.
Credits and asset/movement usage boundaries are in SOURCES.md.

The complete journey, definite choice and consequence, live simulation, scenario
execution, population findings and approved study-record integration remain
unimplemented in the current character test. See ../STATUS.md for the work list.
