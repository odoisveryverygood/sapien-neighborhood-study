One character performance

A ten-second visual study: an offer exceeds a written monthly limit. The person compares the two, moves the offer aside, releases it and looks up across the counter.

The person, $450 limit, $540 offer and response are authored illustrations. No Sapien study result, motive or prediction is asserted.

Character data: Vitruvian / CharMorph, supplied as CC0 in the pinned VitruvianGodot source notice. Separate eyes, FACS facial shapes and the original brow and lash guides are adapted here. The shirt uses a baked cloth simulation.

A restrained torso layer is retimed and attenuated from CMU motion data (subject 22, trial 18). The hands, gaze, expression and paper interaction are authored. No source Mixamo clips are used.

The data used in this project was obtained from mocap.cs.cmu.edu. The database was created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217.
